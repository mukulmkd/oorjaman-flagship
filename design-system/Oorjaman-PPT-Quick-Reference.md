## Colour

| Token | HEX | Slide use |
| --- | --- | --- |

**Wordmark:** Oorja green + Man navy. Never flatten.

**Don’t:** `#2563eb`, slate `#0f172a`, rainbow charts, yellow fills.

## Type

**Plus Jakarta Sans** (400/500/600/700). Fallback: Calibri / Arial.

| Role | Weight | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| TITLE | 600–700 | 24–28 pt (cover 40–48) |
| BODY | 400 | 16–18 pt, 1.4 line |
| KPI number | 700 | 36–44 pt, tracking −3% |
| KPI label | 600 | 11 pt, uppercase, `#516a7b` |
| FOOTER | 500 | 10 pt, `#516a7b` |

Tagline: 10–12 pt, uppercase, tracking ~18%, `#9B9B9B` (or `#516a7b` if contrast fails).

---

## Chart

- Title: 16 pt semibold ink  
- Axis / legend: 11 pt muted  
- Grid: `#c5d9d4` dashed  
- Data: `#1f8660` then `#246488`  
- Money: ₹ with `en-IN`  
- Energy: kW, panels

## Shape

Cards 14px radius · buttons 12px · chips pill · shadows almost none (hairline first).

Icons: outline, 24px, primary or muted. Same weight as Ionicons in the apps.
