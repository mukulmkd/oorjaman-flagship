## Full description

(4000 characters max)

```
OorjaMan Partner is the field app for technicians and vendor teams who clean solar panels with OorjaMan.

See assigned visits, confirm you’re available, and run each job with a clear, outdoor-friendly flow:

• Open your job queue and visit details
• Verify the customer’s Job Start Code before work begins
• Complete the safety checklist
• Track time on site
• Capture before and after photos as proof of service
• Submit the completion report when the visit is done

Built for partners in the field — large tap targets, minimal steps, and a calm, professional interface so you can focus on safe, high-quality cleaning.

OorjaMan connects customers, vendors, and technicians on one platform. Partners stay linked to their vendor, receive assignments from dispatch, and leave photo evidence on every visit.

WE CLEAN. YOU GENERATE.

Need help? Contact your vendor admin or OorjaMan support.
```
