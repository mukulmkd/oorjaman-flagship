## THE BIG VISION

Oorjaman = **energy infrastructure platform**.

1. Solar marketplace — customers find vendors / products / services *(today’s neighbourhood)*  
2. Solar ecosystem — installation + financing + AMC + cleaning + monitoring + insurance + products  
3. Energy platform — solar + batteries + EV charging + efficiency + smart meters + energy management  
4. Energy intelligence — data/AI across thousands/millions of assets  
5. National clean-energy infrastructure — a platform through which India’s distributed-energy economy *could* operate *(ambition, not coverage)*

---

## CONCEPTS IN THIS FILM

2. **Energy Passport** — permanent digital identity per participating asset (capacity, generation, consumption, savings, CO₂, equipment, install date, warranty, AMC/cleaning history, performance, remaining life, finance, insurance, carbon, resale). Transferable with a property. Data compound.

3. **Energy Score** — e.g. 872/1000 from generation, efficiency, grid dependence, battery, equipment health, carbon, consumption, maintenance. Benchmark for homes, apartments, hotels, schools, hospitals, industry, commercial. Category-creating (hotel: 914/1000).

4. **Energy independence** — sell the outcome, not only a plant: solar + battery + smart meter + EMS + EV charger + AMC + insurance + financing.

5–6. **Verified marketplace + Vendor Rating** — not generic. Oorjaman Verified; algorithmic 4.8/5 from installation quality 25%, satisfaction 20%, response 15%, generation 20%, warranty 10%, documentation 10%. Trust.

7–8. **Oorjaman Finance + Solar-as-a-Service** — customer → Oorjaman → bank/NBFC → vendor. Loans, EMI, lease, pay-as-you-save, commercial/MSME/apartment, solar+battery. SaaS: ₹X/month for clean electricity; investor/vendor may own the system.

9–11. **Oorjaman Care + technician network + Academy** — AMC, cleaning, inspection, inverter, thermography, warranty, emergency. Job board (“Uber for solar technicians”). Training → Oorjaman Certified Energy Technician / Certified Professional.

12–14. **AI Energy Advisor, AI monitoring, Energy Health Score** — bill → plan. Expected vs actual (500 vs 390 kWh → 22%). Plant Health 91/100 green / yellow / red.

16–17. **Carbon Wallet / Green Points** and a **Green Property** search layer (Energy Score above 800).

18–19. **Apartments Energy OS** and **Oorjaman for MSMEs** (one dashboard; illustrative factory saving).

20. **Energy Marketplace** — generation, storage, mobility, efficiency (motors, pumps, HVAC, lighting), services, finance (loans, leasing, PPA), technology.

21. **Carbon Intelligence** for corporate ESG.

22. **National Energy Map** — moonshot intelligence layer for governments, DISCOMs, banks, manufacturers, investors. Not present-day coverage.

23–24. **Platform layer + APIs** — do not manufacture every component. Connect manufacturers, financiers, vendors, technicians, customers, energy data. APIs for banks, insurers, OEMs, government, property.

26–27. **Own the customer journey.** Potential platform economy (not current revenue): transaction fee, subscriptions, financing, AMC, cleaning, equipment, insurance, EMS, enterprise SaaS, ESG, certification, advertising, data/API, optimisation. Do not launch all layers at once.
